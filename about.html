Experience

Led by a 25-year operator

Trippix was founded in March 2023 by Mohamed Emam, who has spent 25 years in BPO and call-center operations and employee transport. Earlier in his career he ran transport for 5,300 employees a day, more than 35,000 trips a month. That experience of running transport at scale shapes how Trippix plans and operates every client.
